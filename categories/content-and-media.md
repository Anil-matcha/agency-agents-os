# Content & Media

Umbrella repos covering AI-generated creative production: video, image, and voice.

| Umbrella repo | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`ai-video-agent`](https://github.com/SamurAIGPT/ai-video-agent) | Video generation, avatar/UGC talking-head videos, ad-creative remake | Blueprint |
| [`ai-image-agent`](https://github.com/SamurAIGPT/ai-image-agent) | Image generation, thumbnails, brand content | Blueprint |
| [`ai-voice-agent`](https://github.com/SamurAIGPT/ai-voice-agent) | Narration/dubbing, voice cloning, outbound calling, podcast strategy | Blueprint |
| `ai-content-repurposing-agent` | Clipping and repurposing winning content across formats | Coming Soon |

These are the categories with the most direct API backing today — video, image, and voice generation are live capabilities on [Muapi](https://muapi.ai).
