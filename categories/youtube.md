# YouTube Growth

| Umbrella repo | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`open-ai-youtube-agent`](https://github.com/SamurAIGPT/open-ai-youtube-agent) | Video SEO (titles/descriptions/tags/thumbnails), keyword & tag research, competitor channel analysis, trend discovery, channel analytics — the VidIQ-style toolkit | Blueprint |

Video SEO Optimization is live today, built entirely on capabilities already shipped elsewhere in this catalog (`ai-image-agent` for thumbnails, `ai-seo-agent` for a keyword-data proxy, `ai-social-agent` for the actual YouTube upload). The other four sub-agents are Coming Soon — they depend on YouTube-specific search/video/channel data. Muapi's existing SEO provider (DataForSEO) already sells a full YouTube SERP API family (Organic search rankings, Video Info, Subtitles, Comments) that isn't wired up yet — completing that integration, not a new vendor, would close most of this repo.
