# Go-to-Market

| Umbrella repo | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`ai-gtm-agent`](https://github.com/SamurAIGPT/ai-gtm-agent) | Shared GTM context, strategy routing, cross-channel launch planning, and performance review | Coming Soon |

Go-to-market is a cross-functional umbrella. It coordinates the research,
SEO, marketing, sales, advertising, social, media, analytics, e-commerce,
competitor, and reputation repos rather than duplicating their individual
workflows.

The intended dependency order is:

```text
project context → GTM strategy → launch orchestration → performance review
```

The GTM workflows produce plans, reports, and handoffs. They do not send
outreach, publish content, launch ads, change budgets, edit CRM records, or
modify external properties without a separate human-approved action in the
relevant sibling workflow.

The umbrella is Coming Soon because several cross-functional data surfaces —
research, B2B enrichment, campaign analytics, ad performance, social
analytics, and reputation data — are not yet live across the underlying
capability set.
